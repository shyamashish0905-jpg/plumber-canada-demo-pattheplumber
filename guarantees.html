Before hiring

ASK FOR
THE PAPER.

Any plumber can say they are licensed. A straight answer includes the details. These are fair questions to ask before work starts.

  • What is your licence number, and who issues it?
  • What exactly is included in this price?
  • What could change the price, and will you tell me first?
  • What warranty comes with the work?
  • Who do I contact if something goes wrong?
