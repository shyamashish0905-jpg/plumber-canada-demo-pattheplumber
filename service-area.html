Before the visit

HELP PAT
FIND YOU.

You do not need to diagnose the problem. A few details make the first conversation faster and help Pat decide what to bring.

  • Your town or neighbourhood.
  • The nearest major intersection — not your full address in the first message.
  • One or two photos of the leak, fixture or unit.
  • Whether the water is currently shut off.
  • The best time to reach you.
